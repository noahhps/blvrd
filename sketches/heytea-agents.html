Each agent is told apart by its hair and by what it's doing with its face. Working, the line "boils" like a hand-drawn cartoon and the action plays. When it needs you, it looks up and says "hey!". At rest its eyes are shut, as on HEYTEA's cup; they open while it works or needs you. When it's done, a heart. Shown at 140px, then 40px and 24px.
