Doodle agents

Line-drawn people after HEYTEA's figures, with as few lines as possible: a head, one line for the body, one arm doing the job, and a flat colour prop with no outline. Each agent is told apart by what it's doing, with the prop in its colour, plus an accessory. State uses the same action: idle pauses it, working plays it, "needs you" looks up with a bubble, and done hops and sparks. Small sizes crop to head, shoulders and prop.
